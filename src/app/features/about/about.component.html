Who We Are

A Team Dedicated to Measurable Impact

QLSS Business Consulting was founded with a singular mission to help organisations achieve operational excellence through world-class consulting, training, and auditing solutions. Based in Pune, Maharashtra, we serve clients across industries throughout India and globally, including China, the USA, Hungary, Saudi Arabia, and the UAE.

Our consultants bring decades of hands-on experience in Quality Management Systems, Lean Manufacturing, Sustainability, EHS compliance, and Leadership Development. We don't just advise we partner with you to implement lasting change.

  • Lean Six Sigma ImplementationBlack Belt, Green Belt & Yellow Belt programs
