The “5 Whys” Quick Check

  • Symptom: The TV remote is broken.
  • Why? It fell off the sofa and shattered.
  • Why? It was left balancing on the armrest.
  • Why? It does not have a designated storage spot.
  • Root Cause: Lack of 5S (Set in Order) for living room electronics.
  • Preventive Action: Buy a small remote organizer basket for the coffee table.

Why monitor these specific KPIs?

1. Financial Health: expenses and utilities show budget control; tracking electricity units or gas cylinders also supports EHS awareness, while impulse purchases expose Overproduction / Inventory waste.

2. Guests Visited (Social Capital): keeps the household connected and hospitable rather than operating only like a strict factory.

3. Health Issues & Safety Incidents: sick days, minor cuts, burns or near-misses reveal patterns that may need root-cause analysis.

4. Equipment Breakdowns (TPM): unexpected washing-machine, AC, geyser or appliance failures indicate preventive-maintenance gaps; the target should move toward zero breakdowns.

5. Kaizen Ideas Submitted: turns improvement into a family habit and encourages children to propose solutions instead of only reporting problems.
